# Political Substack research

[Read the working manuscript](https://hunternbh.github.io/substack-political-research/)

Exploratory research on audience claims and editorial authority in political newsletters. The HTML includes the saved-data inventory, provisional political classifications of fifty publications, and the working ASR manuscript. Supporting pages document the expanded alignment review and manual pilot.

All findings and classifications remain provisional. Independent human validation and comparative tests are outstanding. The cohort is a fixed October 2026 ranking and is politically uneven.

This repository contains the public HTML reports and source-linked summaries. The underlying scraped corpus, individual-level coding downloads, billing records, and local archive downloads are not distributed here. Links to those downloads are replaced by local-file notes in the public version. The original research files are preserved locally.
