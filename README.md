# Political Substack research

[Plain-language project overview](https://hunternbh.github.io/substack-political-research/) · [All fifty monthly coverage charts](https://hunternbh.github.io/substack-political-research/Substack_Monthly_Post_Coverage.html) · [Working manuscript](https://hunternbh.github.io/substack-political-research/ASR_Manuscript_Working_Draft.html)

Exploratory research on audience claims and editorial authority in political newsletters. The front page explains what data we have, what they can answer, what remains missing, and the expansion from fifty to one hundred publications. Expansion progress is a dated static checkpoint, not a live feed.

The main page is a report hub with buttons for all twenty HTML reports. Coverage charts are featured first; earlier analyses and saved progress snapshots are grouped separately and marked historical. Every supporting report links back to the hub. Large individual-comment appendices in three older reports remain local.

The monthly coverage report separates restricted article bodies from missing post records and flags empty months. Supporting pages document the provisional political classifications and manual pilot.

All findings and classifications remain provisional. Independent human validation and comparative tests are outstanding. The cohort follows an October 2026 paid U.S. Politics ranking and is politically uneven.

This repository contains public HTML reports, aggregate charts and source-linked summaries. The underlying scraped corpus, individual-level coding downloads, billing records and local archive downloads are not distributed here. The original research files are preserved locally.
