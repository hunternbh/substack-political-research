The same filter, applied to all one hundred

Mark a month when more than half its recorded posts have restricted bodies. Exclude the whole publication if marked months are at least 20% of months with recorded posts. Exactly 50% does not mark a month; exactly 20% excludes a publication. Empty months have undefined shares and do not enter the denominator. The rule does not select publications by political orientation or engagement.
